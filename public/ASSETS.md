# Sample learning assets

The four course photographs under `images/` were generated for this prototype to depict Indian learning settings. They are illustrative images, not photographs of named faculty, actual enrolled students or real customer testimonials.

Fonts are bundled through the `@fontsource/inter`, `@fontsource/plus-jakarta-sans` and `@fontsource/noto-sans-devanagari` packages under their SIL Open Font Licenses. Lucide icons use the ISC licence. PDF rendering uses PDF.js (Apache-2.0). See installed package licence files for exact terms.

The API's `server/assets` workbook, diagram and short revision video are authored sample materials for testing delivery. They are not a full JEE/NEET/UPSC/CBSE curriculum. Replace samples with coaching-owned teaching material before real use.
